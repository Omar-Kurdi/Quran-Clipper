# Quran-Clipper v1.1

The built-in reciters, rebuilt. Every reciter is now the Quran Foundation's recording of each ayah
on its own, so the passage you choose starts and ends exactly where it should -- nothing about an
ayah's boundaries is measured, estimated or corrected any more.

**Try it:** a temporary demo, for testing, runs at **[quran.okurdi.com](https://quran.okurdi.com)**.

## Highlights

- **Built-in reciters recorded ayah by ayah.** Eleven recitations from the
  [Quran Foundation](https://quran.foundation), each published as one file per ayah with word
  timings: Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais, Saud Al-Shuraim, Abu Bakr Al-Shatri, Hani Ar-Rifai, Abdul Basit
  Abdul Samad (Murattal and Mujawwad), Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary (Murattal and Muallim), Mohamed
  Siddiq Al-Minshawi (Murattal and Mujawwad) and Mohamed Al-Tablawi. A passage plays its ayah files
  joined end to end, so every ayah lands where its own recording starts -- to the millisecond.
  Inside a long ayah, captions still change where the reciter pauses or goes back.
  They replace the ten chapter-recording reciters of v1.0, whose ayahs were placed by published
  timings that were right most of the time but not always.
- **New reciters as they are published.** `./start.sh` says when the Quran Foundation adds a
  recitation the studio does not offer yet, and `./add_new_reciters` adds and downloads it.
- **Choose what to download.** `./install.sh` asks whether to download every reciter (about
  24 GB), a few picked from a list, or none; a reciter not downloaded is fetched surah by surah the
  first time each is used, and kept. `--reciters all|qf-3,qf-10` and `--no-recordings` answer it
  unattended.
- **Captions that follow the reciter's breath.** A caption changes in the silence where the voice
  stops, never inside a word, and a long ayah breaks only where the reciter really stopped -- at a
  stop sign or at a breath -- never across a join tajweed carries through. Pauses are now heard by
  the phoneme model as well as by loudness, so a held madd or an echoing room no longer reads as a
  stop, and a stop the loudness missed is caught; Mujawwad recitations, with their long held
  notes, ask for a much longer pause.
- **Highlight as recited, where the reciter is.** Where the published word times run early -- by
  up to six seconds in places -- a word lights up when it is heard.
- **Clips that start and end with the voice.** A passage no longer opens and closes on the
  silence each ayah's recording carries, and a built-in reciter's clip can be trimmed further with
  the timeline's clip handles, as an upload can.
- **Saved projects list:** every column under its own heading, the clip's real length, a title that
  follows the ayahs left after trimming, and the full text of anything cut short on hover.
- **The Quran-Clipper name** at the top of the studio reloads it; the clip in progress is kept as a
  draft and offered back.
- **Fixed:** a translation chosen while another was still loading could stay missing until the
  choice was changed again.
- **Export counts for a studio's owner:** a private `/stats` page counting finished exports (surah,
  length and shape only -- nothing about who made them; the privacy policy says so).

## Upgrading from v1.0

- The v1.0 reciters and their data are gone. Projects made with them keep their captions but not
  their audio; projects made from your own recordings are unaffected.
- Delete what v1.0 kept on disk outside git -- before the first build, because the build reads
  everything inside the project folder and a large folder there runs it out of memory:
  `rm -rf data/audio-cache data/qul/recitations .run/reciters`
- Run `./install.sh` to download the new reciters (into `~/.local/share/quran-clipper/qf-audio/`,
  outside the project; `QC_QF_AUDIO_DIR` moves it), then `./start.sh`.

## Known limits

- **Hafs ʿan ʿĀsim only.**
- Reciters the Quran Foundation publishes only as whole-surah recordings (Maher Al-Muaiqly, Saad
  Al-Ghamdi, Yasser Ad-Dossary and others) are not offered: their ayahs could only be placed by
  timings, which is what this release moved away from. These may be re-introduced in a later
  version.
- Until the studio's Google app passes YouTube's API audit, YouTube keeps uploads private
  whatever was chosen.
