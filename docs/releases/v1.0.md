# Quran Clipper v1.0

The first release of Quran Clipper Studio: a Quran clip and video maker that turns any
recitation into a captioned short video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, in the
browser. Free for personal and non-commercial use (see [LICENSE](../../LICENSE)).

## Highlights

- **Your own recording, timed word by word.** Upload a recitation, audio or video; the
  studio finds the passage and places every word on the audio. It aligns against the known
  Quran text rather than guessing it, so no word can go missing or land in the wrong surah
  ([how it works](../ALIGNMENT.md)). The isti'adha and basmala before a passage get captions of
  their own.
- **Ten built-in reciters,** each with published timings, for a passage in a few clicks.
- **Mushaf typography:** the Madani mushaf's page glyphs, IndoPak and Digital Khatt, with the
  waqf signs on their words.
- **Translations** from 126 editions in any language quran.com publishes, up to three under
  each caption.
- **Captions that follow the recitation:** cross-fades, slides, zooms and soft focus between
  captions, and words revealed or highlighted as they are recited.
- **Every platform's frame:** 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9, several at once, up to 4K, rendered in
  the browser with WebCodecs.
- **Straight to YouTube,** private, unlisted, public or scheduled, signed in once per browser.
- **English and Arabic interface,** eight themes, a guided tour on the first visit, and a phone
  layout with the same three tabs.

## Getting started

See [Quick start](../../README.md#quick-start): `./install.sh` sets up the studio and the
alignment sidecar, and `./start.sh` runs them. `./install.sh --public --domain example.com`
sets up a public studio behind HTTPS.

## Known limits

- **Hafs ʿan ʿĀsim only.**
- Until the studio's Google app passes YouTube's API audit, YouTube keeps uploads private
  whatever was chosen.
