# Quran-Clipper v1.2

More reciters, and captions that follow them more closely. The Quran Foundation's whole-surah
recitations are back, cut here into one file per ayah, and the studio now shows a reciter going
back over what he has read as captions of their own.

**Try it:** a temporary demo, for testing, runs at **[quran.okurdi.com](https://quran.okurdi.com)**.

## Highlights

- **Nine more reciters.** The recitations the [Quran Foundation](https://quran.foundation) publishes
  only as whole-surah recordings -- Maher Al-Muaiqly, Saad Al-Ghamdi, Ahmed Al-Ajmy, Yasser
  Ad-Dossary, Khalifa Al-Tunaiji, Abdullah Ali Jabir, Bandar Baleela, Abdullah Abu Sharida and
  Ahmed Tahoun (beta) -- are cut into one file per ayah, so a passage starts and ends where its
  ayahs do, as the ayah-by-ayah reciters always have. A join is kept only where the recording
  bears it out: a pause, the Foundation's timings, or the two ayahs read again on their own. A join
  nothing stands by is kept as unsure, and only a passage starting or ending there is refused.
  Al-Muaiqly, Al-Ghamdi and Al-Ajmy are cut in full (114 of 114 surahs each) and checked by ear;
  the others are cut the first time a surah is used, or ahead of time with `./cut_reciters`.
- **Repeats as captions of their own.** Where a reciter goes back over ayahs he has read -- one or
  ten of them, or the ayah he has just finished -- each pass is captioned as he reads it and marked
  as a repeat: ↻ on the timeline and in the Inspector, and, unless turned off in Style, in the
  video. A caption can be marked or unmarked as a repeat by hand.
- **The ayah's number where the mushaf puts it.** By default a caption that reaches its ayah's end
  closes with the mushaf's ayah ornament (۝ with the number inside), in every Arabic face,
  IndoPak Nastaleeq included, and its translation with "(n)" -- part of the editable text, so a
  long ayah split over captions shows it only where you leave it. The number between the cards is
  still a choice in Style.
- **Edit audio**, for built-in reciters and your own recordings: cut or shorten any stretch, shorten
  pauses, speed up without changing the reciter's voice, even out loudness, clean up a recording
  of your own, and end it ready to loop (on by default).
- **Captions that follow the recitation more closely.** A caption breaks at a stop the reciter
  makes even where no sign marks it, when the recording shows a real stop; a short phrase said
  again after a breath gets a caption of its own; two ayahs read in one breath are both captioned;
  and a word listed for no time at all no longer flashes up as a caption.
- **Recordings kept in step with the Quran Foundation** through its Content Sync APIs, at least
  weekly, as its Developer Terms require.
- **Credits:** the description and caption the studio writes for a clip credit the Quran
  Foundation by name. Nothing is ever added to the video itself.
- **Saved projects list** shows the last 50 projects.
- **Fixed:** a passage whose recording had changed could play its old audio for up to a day.

## Upgrading from v1.1

- Run `npm run db:push` once: projects gain the ayah-number and repeat-mark settings.
- `./install.sh` offers the new reciters; a chapter reciter is cut on your GPU (the alignment
  service is needed), surah by surah as it is used, or all at once with `./cut_reciters`.
- Rebuild and restart: `./stop.sh && ./start.sh`.

## Known limits

- **Hafs ʿan ʿĀsim only.**
- Of the chapter reciters, only Al-Muaiqly, Al-Ghamdi and Al-Ajmy have been checked by ear so far.
- Where a reciter weeps or holds a note very long, a repeat can still show a short stray caption.
- Until the studio's Google app passes YouTube's API audit, YouTube keeps uploads private
  whatever was chosen.
